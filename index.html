Selected Publications

First authorPreprint

Quantum Wavefunction Augmentation via Variational Autoencoders (Q-WAVE)

arXiv:2609.07972, 2026

A β-annealed variational autoencoder, seeded with IBM Heron hardware samples and CISD determinants, learns the wavefunction’s structure and expands the sample-based quantum diagonalization subspace beyond what the hardware measured. Reaches sub-millihartree accuracy against full CI and scales to a 60-qubit Cr2 stress test.
